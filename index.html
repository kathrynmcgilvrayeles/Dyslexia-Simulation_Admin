Understanding Dyslexia at Student Services

For students with dyslexia, dense enrolment forms, USI codes, or stark white emails can appear reversed, crowded, or shifting on the page. Simple accommodations—like tinted overlays, plain-English spacing, and supportive front-counter help—can stabilise visual processing and ease enrolment stress.
